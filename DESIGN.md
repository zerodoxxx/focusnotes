# Design

Last Updated: 2026-10-18 (previous commit: `962ea7d78abc0298c7d1a66063a3d24476c95d8e`)

## Product surfaces

focusnotes runs as a macOS accessory app and puts its always-available commands in a status-bar menu. The menu includes selection and area capture, note search, session control, quick-note writing, subheading entry, the notes folder, semantic-model controls, permission links, an optional updater item, and Quit. ([Sources/App/main.swift](Sources/App/main.swift#L6-L7), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L1020-L1069))

The default global shortcuts are ⌥⇧C for selection capture, ⌥⇧S for area capture, ⌥⇧F for search, ⌥⇧A for session control, ⌥⇧N for a quick note, ⌥⇧H for a subheading, and ⌥⇧L for the notes folder. ([Sources/CaptureKit/Hotkeys.swift](Sources/CaptureKit/Hotkeys.swift#L49-L55)) These are registered as exclusive Carbon hotkeys; if registration fails, the menu keeps the command, removes its shortcut indication, and exposes a retry command with a reason in the tooltip. ([Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L218-L243), [Sources/CaptureKit/Hotkeys.swift](Sources/CaptureKit/Hotkeys.swift#L89-L121), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L1087-L1111))

The interactive surfaces are borderless, nonactivating panels: they borrow keyboard focus while leaving the app inactive, and they cannot become the main window. Passive toast panels ignore mouse events. ([Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L39-L64), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L66-L76)) Interactive panels without a saved movable position are centered on the pointer's screen, with their top edge 22% of the visible screen height below the top; toasts are placed 72 points above the visible bottom. ([Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L210-L223), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L137-L149))

### Sessions

Starting a session with a bare name resumes its only matching note at the existing path, or creates a new top-level note under `Sessions/` when there is no match. Duplicate bare-name matches open a picker with each note's title, path, and modification date, newest first; Up/Down selects, Return resumes the selected path, and Escape cancels. A slash-qualified name such as `archive/Standup` resolves directly under `Sessions/` and skips the picker. ([Sources/Core/SessionStore.swift](Sources/Core/SessionStore.swift), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift), [Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift))

### Quick note

The quick note is a floating text editor with a centered title, a 38-point top band, a 38-point footer, and a 20-point text inset. Its five top-band buttons close the editor, invoke “Open as Note,” browse notes, start a new note, or show Actions; the footer shows the save shortcut, Actions shortcut, and Escape hint. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L366-L375), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L472-L474), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L481-L485), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L961-L969), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L84-L103), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L68-L74))

At rest, the top-band controls and footer are transparent and inactive, while the title is drawn at 30% opacity; pointer entry or a held reveal state shows the controls and footer and raises the title to 60% opacity. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L488-L495), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L104-L105), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L542-L560), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L565-L582), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L607-L611), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L86-L100)) The close and open buttons sit at the left edge; Browse, New Note, and Actions sit in a capsule at the right. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L84-L103), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L120-L140))

The title is the destination name when one is selected, otherwise it comes from the first 2,000 characters of nonblank note text through the app's note-title rule, with “New note” as the empty fallback. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L515-L528), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L750-L753), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L92-L94)) The footer labels the save action “Create note” or “Add to note” according to the destination; a restored-draft label is shown only if the footer still fits. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L505-L514), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L530-L540))

When no session or Browse destination is active, the first nonblank line supplies the saved note name. Valid unprefixed slash-separated names identify folders under the vault; a leading `/` resolves from HOME. For relative names, a URL-shaped form, a trailing or repeated slash, or whitespace adjacent to a slash uses the legacy flat filename cleanup; malformed leading-slash HOME paths remain validation errors. Other explicit path validation errors remain visible instead of being converted to a generic filename error. The full path is resolved before display truncation; the quick-note title shows the final component with the existing 60-character cap, and an existing matching file receives an appended typed note. Missing folders are created and existing folder/file casing is reused without regard to case. An active session still directs typed notes to its `Sessions/` note. ([Sources/Core/TypedNote.swift](Sources/Core/TypedNote.swift), [Sources/Core/NoteTitle.swift](Sources/Core/NoteTitle.swift), [Sources/Core/CaptureService.swift](Sources/Core/CaptureService.swift), [Sources/Core/Vault.swift](Sources/Core/Vault.swift), [Sources/Core/NoteWriter.swift](Sources/Core/NoteWriter.swift))

HOME-relative notes enter search and Browse only through exact paths in the durable external-note registry; Focusnotes does not scan HOME. The registry caches a validated path snapshot and generation, invalidated when its file or parent changes. FSEvents observes only the vault root. Registered external files and their immediate parents use bounded vnode sources, with missing parent paths observed from their nearest existing ancestor; callbacks recheck only the registered paths. A malformed, unsupported, or unsafe registry is preserved and disables external notes with a warning until a valid registry is restored. Images in nested or external notes keep relative Markdown links back to the selected vault's `assets/` directory. ([Sources/App/CapturePipeline.swift](Sources/App/CapturePipeline.swift), [Sources/Core/ExternalNoteRegistry.swift](Sources/Core/ExternalNoteRegistry.swift), [Sources/Core/Vault.swift](Sources/Core/Vault.swift), [Sources/Core/VaultWatcher.swift](Sources/Core/VaultWatcher.swift), [Sources/Core/NoteWriter.swift](Sources/Core/NoteWriter.swift#L88-L95), [Sources/Core/CaptureFormat.swift](Sources/Core/CaptureFormat.swift#L163-L189))

The editor renders Markdown while editing: headings, unordered and ordered lists, task lists, block quotes, bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code, links, and fenced code receive distinct typography or decoration. ([Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownStyler.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownStyler.swift#L149-L175), [Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownStyler.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownStyler.swift#L177-L224), [Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownStyler.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownStyler.swift#L226-L263)) Markdown markers are hidden outside the active editing or composition range, and become visible when the selection enters the corresponding element. ([Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownLayoutManager.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownLayoutManager.swift#L27-L49), [Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownLayoutManager.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownLayoutManager.swift#L198-L225), [Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownLayoutManager.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/MarkdownLayoutManager.swift#L261-L276)) Clicking a rendered task checkbox toggles its stored marker; Return continues list markers, numbers ordered lists, and Tab/Shift-Tab indents or outdents list lines. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L160-L193), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L294-L340))

The editor saves with ⌘Return, toggles Actions with ⌘K, and maps ⌘P to Browse, ⌘N to New Note, ⌘O to Open as Note, ⇧⌘P to pin, ⇧⌘C to copy Markdown, and ⇧⌘Delete to discard the draft. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L202-L224), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L95-L103), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L144-L156)) Escape closes an open card or the editor; ⌘K closes Actions or switches from Browse to Actions. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L634-L642), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L731-L752), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L210-L218))

The note begins at 400 points wide and can grow to 600 points based on content or footer width; its text viewport has a two-line minimum and a cap derived from the lower of 60% of the visible screen height or 680 points, less the top and bottom bands. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L68-L70), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L97-L101), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L926-L949)) Longer text scrolls, and width only grows while the panel remains open. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L926-L945)) The editor can be dragged from its top or bottom band and remembers its position as fractions of the visible screen; it remains open when it resigns key or the active Space changes, while other interactive panels dismiss when they resign key. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L583-L589), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L1048-L1050), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L10-L16), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L39-L64), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L157-L181), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L226-L234), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L251-L267))

### Actions and Browse cards

Actions and Browse appear as one card below the quick note's top band. Opening one closes the other, and the transparent window extends downward to fit the card without changing the note's visible size. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L409-L423), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L630-L657), [Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L306-L315)) Both cards have a filter field and a separator above their content. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L25-L54))

Actions contains New Note, Add/Remove from Pinned, Open as Note, Copy as Markdown, and Discard Draft; copy and discard are disabled when the editor is empty. Rows show symbols and boxed shortcut glyphs, and the actions are divided into groups. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L3-L22), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L140-L172)) Its field filters titles without case sensitivity; Up/Down moves among enabled rows, Return runs the selection, and Escape or ⌘K closes the card. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L210-L227), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L250-L265))

Browse filters note titles without case sensitivity, shows pinned notes before recent notes, builds at most 200 rows, and displays up to seven rows before scrolling. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L78-L100), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L152-L183), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteOverlayTokens.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteOverlayTokens.swift#L47-L61), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L718-L725)) Rows show a symbol, title, detail, and a pin symbol when pinned; the footer labels Return as Write here, ⌘Return as Open, and ⌥Return as Reveal. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L13-L27), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L73-L75)) Up/Down selects a row, Return chooses its current destination action, and a click selects that note as the editor's write destination. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L122-L150), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L160-L163), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L728-L737))

### Search and input panels

Search uses a 640-point-wide panel with a search field, loading indicator, and a scrollable results list beneath a Recent or Results heading when rows exist; its footer shows Copy, Open, Show in Finder, and Close. ([Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L48-L80), [Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L110-L118), [Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L138-L148), [Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L191-L229), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L53-L61), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L894-L920)) It displays up to eight results at a time. Rows carry a title and snippet with optional date/source-app metadata; text and note rows use symbols, while image rows display a thumbnail when asset loading succeeds. Matching is emphasized in snippets. ([Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L120-L128), [Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L138-L189), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L31-L34), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L135-L137), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L153-L154), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L942-L967), [Sources/OverlayKit/SharedViews.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SharedViews.swift#L237-L240)) An empty query shows up to 20 recent capture blocks; a nonempty query fuses keyword and semantic results. ([Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L894-L924), [Sources/Core/Index/SearchIndex.swift](Sources/Core/Index/SearchIndex.swift#L204-L216)) Up/Down changes selection, Return copies, ⌘Return opens the note, and ⌥Return reveals it in Finder. ([Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L314-L338), [Sources/App/AppController.swift](Sources/App/AppController.swift#L930-L937))

Input panels use a 480-point-wide text field with optional context labels and up to six suggestions. ([Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift#L12-L30), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L53-L61), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L135-L137)) Up/Down selects a suggestion, Tab inserts it into the field and refreshes suggestions, and Return accepts the selection or submits the entered text. ([Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift#L140-L167))

### Toast feedback

Toasts are passive panels with a 56-point height and content-based width between 240 and 380 points; their icons distinguish success, warning, session, and copied feedback. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift#L28-L50), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L137-L176)) The default display time is two seconds and warnings last three seconds unless a call site overrides the duration; showing a new toast immediately replaces the previous toast, and the panel announces the toast title to accessibility clients. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift#L15-L18), [Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift#L79-L103), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L175-L176))

## Visual system and accessibility

`OverlayTheme` holds the shared dimensions, colors, fonts, symbols, and motion timings. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L4-L29), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L30-L46), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L62-L74), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L136-L176)) Overlay surfaces combine an active `.popover` visual-effect material with a high-opacity tint and a half-point border; the tint, border, and accent colors resolve for light and dark appearance. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L4-L29), [Sources/OverlayKit/SharedViews.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SharedViews.swift#L20-L58)) The shared radii are 14 points for panels, 16 for the quick note, and 12 for toasts; text uses system label colors, with secondary and tertiary colors for supporting text. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L10-L22), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L40-L46))

The editor uses 15-point system text with 1.25 line spacing, 13.5-point monospaced code, and semibold heading sizes of 22, 19, 17, and 15 points. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L62-L67)) Its insertion caret and task-checkbox accent use a custom dynamic coral accent color with separate light and dark values. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L23-L27), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L111-L129)) Interactive panels animate in with a short fade and scale, while toasts move upward slightly as they fade in; Reduce Motion skips the scale and toast-travel effects, while opacity fades remain. ([Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/OverlayPanel.swift#L84-L106), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L141-L149), [Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L187-L187))

Accessibility semantics are assigned to individual controls: the editor text view is labeled “Note text,” quick-note chrome buttons have labels and tooltips, and Actions and Browse rows expose button roles and labels. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L454-L455), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteChrome.swift#L19-L34), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteActionMenu.swift#L140-L156), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L13-L30)) Search result rows expose button roles and selected values, while the toast is a static text element and posts an accessibility announcement. ([Sources/OverlayKit/SharedViews.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SharedViews.swift#L182-L212), [Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift#L46-L50), [Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Toast.swift#L95-L103))

The quick note adjusts its height to content and screen height, and its Browse card limits visible rows and scrolls overflow. ([Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteEditorPanel.swift#L926-L949), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L78-L100), [Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/NoteBrowseView.swift#L192-L219)) Search and input panel layouts use fixed widths of 640 and 480 points respectively. ([Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/Theme.swift#L53-L61), [Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/SearchPanel.swift#L77-L83), [Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift](Sources/OverlayKit/InputPanel.swift#L25-L30))
